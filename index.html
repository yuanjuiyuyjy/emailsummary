EmailSummary

EmailSummary 是一個個人自用的小工具：它在擁有者自己的電腦上執行，讀取（唯讀）擁有者本人信箱的新信件，整理成每日摘要供本人閱讀。

EmailSummary is a personal, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computer, reads (read-only) new messages in the owner's own mailbox, and produces a daily digest for the owner to read.

這個工具不對外提供服務，也沒有其他使用者。 It is not offered as a service and has no other users.
