隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

最後更新 Last updated: 2026-10-03

中文

EmailSummary 是個人自用工具，唯一的使用者就是它的擁有者本人。

  • 存取的資料：擁有者本人 Gmail 信箱中的信件（唯讀）。本工具沒有寄信、修改或刪除信件的權限。
  • 用途：只用來產生每日信件摘要，供擁有者本人閱讀。
  • 儲存：授權憑證與摘要檔只存在擁有者自己的電腦上。
  • 第三方處理：為了產生摘要，信件內容會透過擁有者本人的帳號送交 AI 模型服務（Anthropic Claude）處理。除此之外，資料不會提供、出售或轉移給任何其他人。
  • 撤銷：擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式存取權」頁面撤銷授權。

English

EmailSummary is a personal tool whose only user is its owner.

  • Data accessed: messages in the owner's own Gmail mailbox (read-only). The tool has no permission to send, modify, or delete mail.
  • Use: solely to produce a daily email digest for the owner to read.
  • Storage: authorization tokens and digest files are kept only on the owner's own computer.
  • Third-party processing: to produce the digest, message content is processed by an AI model service (Anthropic Claude) through the owner's own account. Beyond that, data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone.
  • Revocation: the owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account third-party access page.
